# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Target Audience

Android power users, enthusiasts, and device owners looking for clean, reliable custom ROM builds (LineageOS, YAAP), installation guides, device specifications, and direct community support.

## Core Value & Positioning

A direct, bloat-free portal by developer and ROM maintainer Grepfox ("Yet another person who loves FOSS"). Provides official/unofficial ROM releases, hardware specs, verified step-by-step flashing guides, and direct channels for developer contact and community discussion.

## Key Surfaces & Features

1. **Home (`index.html`)**: Name, one-line bio and social links only (Email `grepfox@tutamail.com`, Telegram `@grepfox`, support group `@grepfox_chat`, GitHub `@grepfox`), plus links to Releases and FAQ. Device listings live on Releases.
2. **Blog Index (`blog.html`)**: Directory of guides, release notes, and FAQs with authentic device imagery and metadata.
3. **LineageOS for OnePlus Nord 5 (`lexus-22.html`)**: Permanent redirect to official LineageOS documentation at `wiki.lineageos.org/devices/lexus/`.
4. **LineageOS for OnePlus Nord 6 (`nord-6.html`)**: Hardware specs (Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, 165Hz AMOLED, massive battery, LineageOS 24.0) for codename "volkswagen", download/changelog portal, and step-by-step flashing instructions.
5. **YAAP for Poco X3 Pro (`yaap-changelogs.html`)**: Permanent redirect to official YAAP portal at `yaaprom.org/device?codename=vayu`.
6. **FAQ (`blog-faq.html`)**: Cryptographic signature verification, release cycles, contribution guidelines, and community contact.
7. **Contact (`contact.html`)**: Minimal, accessible direct reach-out options.

## Brand Commitments & Voice

- Tone: Minimal, pragmatic, technical, honest, and community-focused.
- No ads, tracking bloat, excessive animations, or heavy frameworks.
- Fast loading, accessible, fully responsive across mobile and desktop.
- Complete dark and light theme support with persistent preference.
