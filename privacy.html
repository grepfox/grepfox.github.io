Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026.

The short version

This site does not use cookies, does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information. It counts visits with privacy-friendly analytics so I know which pages are useful. That's it.

Analytics

Pages load a small script from Cabin, a cookie-free analytics service. Cabin records anonymous, aggregate data such as page views, the referring site, browser type, screen size and country. It does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not build profiles or track you across sites. See Cabin's privacy policy for details.

If you use an ad or tracker blocker, the script may be blocked. The site works the same either way.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data, including your IP address, when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. I don't have access to those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it sticks between visits. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party content

The local manifest generator loads fonts from Google Fonts and a background image from Unsplash, which means those services receive your IP address when you open that page. Download links, Telegram, GitHub and other external sites have their own privacy policies once you leave this site.

Contact and changes

If you email me or message me on Telegram, I only use what you send to reply. Questions about this policy go to grepfox@tutamail.com. If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
