Releases FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Build signing, update cycles and how to contribute.

Are the builds signed?

Yes. Public releases are signed with private release keys, not the generic AOSP test keys. That keeps builds verifiable and lets you update cleanly from month to month.

How often are updates published?

Stable builds usually land monthly, after the Android Security Bulletin merges and LineageOS branch syncs. Test builds go out more often in the Telegram test channels.

How can I contribute?

Pull requests to the device and kernel trees on GitHub are welcome. You can also help by testing builds and filing bug reports with a logcat (adb logcat -d).

Where do I get help if an install fails?

If something goes wrong while unlocking, flashing or updating, ask in the Telegram support group. Both I and the community can help.

How do I reach you directly?

Message me on Telegram at @grepfox or email grepfox@tutamail.com.
